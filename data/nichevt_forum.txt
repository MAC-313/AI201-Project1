3/5 stars

There are A LOT of stuck up students that think they are all that because their parents are well off. Food tastes good, but is extremely over priced. As of Dec 2025, it's $10.25 for a small premade deli wrap on campus. For that price I can get a bowl at Chipotle (which can be 2 meals). Overall, the professors are hit or miss. Northern VA Comm College has a higher rate of good professors. Overall Blacksburg housing is the same or higher than the cost of housing in Northern VA. Parking sucks and doesn't make sense. They don't even have dedicated parking for the on campus health center. It seems like VT is more focused on taking money from you rather than being a good school. They have a saying "No Hokie left behind", but they will leave you behind in the blink of an eye. I was expecting WAY more from this school. I don't know if this is a representation of this country, but this school has no soul. It sold it long ago.
