Forget lukewarm meatloaf and limp salads. These days, college dining halls are serving high-quality, inventive food, much to students' delight.  

And no school has taken campus dining more seriously than Virginia Tech. The university is regularly near the top of The Princeton Review's "best campus food" list, and was no. 1 in 2014.  

VT students ("Hokies") have access to multiple dining centers from marketplace-style West End Market to Hokie Grill, home to Chick-fil-A, Pizza Hut, Carvel, and Dunkin Donuts.
